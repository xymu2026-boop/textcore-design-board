# Colloquial Cleaning Rules

- Remove meaningless fillers, repeated starts, verbal padding, and dense hesitation words when they do not carry teaching value.
- Remove classroom management and logistics by default, such as roll call, microphone reminders, screen sharing, upload reminders, and order keeping.
- Preserve the teacher's explanation order, reasoning chain, examples, conclusions, and transitions that affect meaning.
- Preserve student answers only when the teacher's later explanation depends on them, when they show a typical mistake, or when they contain important content.
- Do not invent missing steps. If the transcript is unclear, keep a concise note in `review_flags`.
- Keep the cleaned text faithful to the source. Improve punctuation and readability, but do not add unsupported facts.
